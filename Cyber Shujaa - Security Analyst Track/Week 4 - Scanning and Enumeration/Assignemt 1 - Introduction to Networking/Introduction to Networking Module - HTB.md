# Introduction to Networking Module
## Introduction
This module covers Networking from a borader perspective. Some of the areas covered 
include but not limited to:

- Network types such as GAN (Global Area Network ), WAN (Wide Area Network), MAN (Metropolitan Area Network), LAN (Local Area Network), VLAN (Virtual LAN), WLAN (Wireless LAN) and VPNs (Virtual Private Network).

- Different network topoligies such as mesh, star, hybrid and more were also covered in the room. Different types of proxies were also covered in the module.

- Network flow, that is, network models were covered in the room. They inculude ISO Open Systems Interconnect (OSI) Model and TCP/IP model. This models illustrate how data flows from one node to another.

- Different addressing methods were also discussed in the module. They include IPv4, IPv6 and MAC addresses. Another key concept covered was subnetting, dividing a bigger network into smaller networks.

- Network protocols, which was a quite overwheling list, were introduced in the module. For this I had to download a cheat sheet to use as a reference guide in my cyber security endevours.

- The last part covered was how nodes establish and maintain connections during data transmission/communication. Different cryptographic mechanisms were discussed, they help maintain integrity and confidentiality of data as it flows from one to another on the internet.

Question: Submit the decimal representation of the subnet mask from the following CIDR: 10.200.20.0/27 <br>
Answer: **255.255.255.224** <br>

Question: Submit the broadcast address of the following CIDR: 10.200.20.0/27 <br>
Answer: **10.200.20.31** <br>

Question: Split the network 10.200.20.0/27 into 4 subnets and submit the network address of the 3rd subnet as the answer. <br>
Answer: **10.200.20.16** <br>

Question: Split the network 10.200.20.0/27 into 4 subnets and submit the broadcast address of the 2nd subnet as the answer. <br>
